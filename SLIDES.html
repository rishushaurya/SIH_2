06 / 08

Cryptographic Consent & Immutability

Strict compliance with DPDP Act 2023 Section 6 (Consent) & Section 8 (Auditing).

DEPA Consent Artifacts
Machine-readable JWT signed via ECDSA P-256. Defines exact purpose, requested fields, 15m expiration, and one-click revocation URL.
Hash-Chain Audit Ledger
Every access links cryptographically to the previous block: SHA256(Block_N-1 + Data). Mathematical fraud detection built-in.
Citizen Privacy Dashboard
Citizens inspect all historical data accesses, view timestamps, and execute immediate one-click consent revocation.
Legal Mandate: Aligned with May 2027 DPDP Full Enforcement Deadline
